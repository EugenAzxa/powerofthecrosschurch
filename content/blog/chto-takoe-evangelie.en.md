title: What is the Gospel? What we preach

---
The word "Gospel" means "good news". It is the news of what God has done for us through Jesus Christ. It is what we preach every Sunday, and it is where faith begins.

> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
> John 3:16

## The Word of God is the foundation

We believe that the Bible, and the Bible alone, is truly the Word of God (2 Timothy 3:16). So everything preached from the pulpit is tested by Scripture. The Bible holds the answers to the questions people carry.

## Who Jesus Christ is

Jesus Christ is the Son of God. On earth He was at once fully God and fully man, and He was the only Man who lived without sin (John 1:1, 1:14).

## The Cross and the resurrection

The blood Jesus Christ shed on the Cross at Calvary washes us from all sin: He took the sins of the whole world upon Himself (1 John 1:7). On the third day after His death He rose from the dead (Luke 24:36-39). The apostle Paul calls this the first thing of all:

> ...how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; and that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures.
> 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

## Salvation is a gift

No one can earn salvation by good works. We are saved by grace, through faith in Jesus Christ:

> For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.
> Ephesians 2:8-9

## How to receive this news

The Gospel calls us to repentance - to turn from sin and come to Jesus Christ for forgiveness (Acts 3:19). A Christian is someone who has asked the Lord Jesus to come into their life, and who rests their hope on what Christ has done for them (John 1:12).
