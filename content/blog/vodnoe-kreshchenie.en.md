Water baptism is one of the foundations of our faith. The Word of God says that those who believe in the Lord Jesus Christ should be baptized in water in the name of Jesus Christ.

## The command of Jesus

Before His ascension Jesus Christ gave His disciples a command the church still carries out:

> Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.
> Matthew 28:19

## Repentance and baptism

On the day of Pentecost, when the people asked the apostles what they should do, Peter answered:

> Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.
> Acts 2:38

Repentance comes before baptism - turning from sin and coming to Jesus Christ for forgiveness. Baptism is not a way to earn salvation: salvation is God's gift, by grace through faith (Ephesians 2:8-9). Baptism is the response of faith and obedience to the Lord.

## Who can be baptized

Anyone who has believed in Jesus Christ and repented. Baptism is a conscious decision by someone who wants to follow Christ.
