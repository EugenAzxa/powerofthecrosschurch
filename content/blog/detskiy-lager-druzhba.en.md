Every summer Power of the Cross runs Druzhba ("Friendship"), a Christian camp for children. It is five days on the shore of Rainy Lake, north of Toronto - a time for children to enjoy the outdoors, make friends and learn about God.

## The camp at a glance

- **Ages:** 7 to 15
- **Length:** 5 days
- **Group size:** 20-30 children
- **Location:** Rainy Lake, north of Toronto

## What the program includes

Bible lessons, sports and games, swimming, relay races, evening activities, hikes in the forest, raft building and crafts.

The Bible says: "Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it" (Proverbs 22:6). Camp is one of the ways children learn the truths of the Bible from an early age and see faith lived out by the adults around them.

## What to pack

- Several changes of underwear
- Comfortable shoes for hot and cool weather, sandals, running shoes
- A sweater and jacket for cold or rainy weather
- Track suits, shorts, T-shirts
- A swimsuit, swim cap and goggles
- A sun hat
- Toothbrush and toothpaste, soap, washcloth, comb, shampoo
- Sunscreen and insect repellent
- Sleeping bag, air mattress, pillow, blanket
- A flashlight
- A separate bag for dirty laundry
- A reusable mug

Please leave expensive jewellery - gold earrings, chains, rings - at home.

## How to register your child

Call the church on **416-858-9317** and we will tell you the dates of the next session and how to sign up. More about the camp is on the [Camp](camp.html) page.
